My WiFi

Turns a paper exercise book of WiFi voucher codes into a proper point-of-sale app — built for a real problem in Gideon's own village.

View live site ↗

When Gideon returned to his home village, the local MTN network was unstable, so vendors had started reselling WiFi access — tracking every voucher code by hand in an exercise book, crossing each one out as it sold. My WiFi replaces that book: photograph a sheet of codes and it reads them automatically, then tracks stock, sales, and earnings from there.

Dashboard
Available codes
Settings
Core features
  • Photograph a sheet of codes — read automatically, on-device
  • Review and correct any misread codes before they're saved
  • Live stock count, all-time sales, and all-time earnings
  • Low-stock warning at a threshold you set
  • Full sold-history log
Design choices
  • Local-first — codes and sales never leave the phone
  • No account, no subscription, free forever
  • Manual backup/restore file instead of a cloud database
  • Optional PIN lock against casual snooping
  • Configurable currency label — not locked to one country

SchoolPay GH

School fees, payments and records in one place: parents pay from their phone, and school admins see everything as it comes in.

View live site ↗

A fee-payment platform for schools in Ghana. Each school gets its own space with its own fees, classes and admins. Students sign up with an access code from their school, pay online through Paystack, and download receipts.

For students and parents
  • Sign up with a school access code, using email or Google
  • See fees for the current term and pay securely from a phone
  • Download payment receipts
  • Open term results once the school uploads them
For school admins
  • Overview of money collected, weekly and by fee
  • Searchable payment list with spreadsheet download
  • Add classes and students by typing or importing a .csv or .xlsx file
  • See who has paid and who hasn't, and remind parents on WhatsApp
  • School profile, term fees and extra admins managed in one place
